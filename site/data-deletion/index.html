Information that may be covered

A request can ask Bytewise to locate and delete information it holds about you, such as:

  • public post details or discussion information processed by a Bytewise workflow;
  • public profile or creator information associated with a result;
  • persona, topic, generated-content, queue, or publishing records that identify you; and
  • other personal information processed through the Bytewise Meta API integration.

Bytewise cannot remove posts from Threads or control data held independently by Meta. Some records may also be held in workflow-execution history or by service providers; the scope of a deletion request depends on what Bytewise can locate and control, applicable law, and the relevant service configuration.

What to include in your email

To help Bytewise identify the relevant information, include only what is reasonably necessary:

  • your name or the name you use on the relevant account;
  • the platform and public username, if relevant;
  • links to posts or profiles, if available;
  • an approximate date or description of the information; and
  • the email address or other safe way to contact you about the request.

Do not send your password, access token, or other login credentials. Bytewise may request limited additional information if reasonably needed to verify or clarify a request. Do not include sensitive information that is not needed to locate the data.

How requests are handled

  1. Bytewise reviews the email and the information provided.
  2. Bytewise may ask for proportionate information to verify identity or locate relevant records.
  3. Bytewise assesses what information it holds and can delete, including relevant operational records and service-provider processing.
  4. Bytewise processes the request as required by applicable law and explains any material limits or information it cannot delete.

Requests are handled by email and manual review. There is no automated deletion callback. No automatic deletion or specific completion time is promised by this draft.

Revoke Meta app permissions

You can also remove the Bytewise integration from your Meta account settings. Look in Meta Accounts Center or the relevant Apps and Websites or Business Integrations settings, select the Bytewise app, and remove its access. The exact menu name and location may vary by account and product; check Meta's current help information if you cannot find the setting.

Revoking permissions prevents future authorized API access where that revocation applies, but it does not by itself send Bytewise a deletion request for information already processed. Email Bytewise separately if you want us to review data deletion.

Need to make a request?

Email bytewiserpersona@yahoo.com with the subject “Data deletion request.”
