BYTEWISE META DEVELOPER APP

Privacy Policy

How Bytewise may access, use, and manage information through its internal social-content automation system.

Last updated: 16 November 2026

IN THIS POLICY The service Information processed How information is used Service providers Storage and retention Credentials and security Your choices and rights Contact

1. The service

The Bytewise Meta Developer App is an API integration used by Bytewise's internal n8n-based automation system. It is not a consumer-facing mobile or web application. The system supports research, content review, scheduling, and publishing for social media personas.

Threads Trend Discovery is being developed and tested. Its availability depends on Meta authorization and supported API capabilities. This policy does not represent that the feature is fully operational or that Meta has approved access.

2. Information processed

When authorized Meta API access is available, a Threads discovery workflow may process public search results and related information, such as:

  • public post text and relevant discussion topics;
  • post URLs and identifiers;
  • public usernames or creator information when needed;
  • publication timestamps; and
  • public engagement information, only where available and authorized.

The system also uses API credentials and access tokens to authenticate connected accounts and authorized requests. Its broader operational workflows may process persona information, approved topics, generated content, content queue records, publishing statuses, and related operational data.

3. How information is used

Public Threads discussions may be evaluated for relevant or emerging topics and persona fit. Where supported and authorized, the planned workflow uses official keyword search with TOP or RECENT results. Bytewise does not assume an unrestricted official trending-topics endpoint exists and does not use scraping to bypass Meta access controls.

Selected topic information may be used for research and verification, then to inform original AI-assisted content. Proposed content is subject to Bytewise's review and quality checks before it enters scheduling or publishing workflows. Threads discovery and its production status remain under development and testing.

4. Service providers and sharing

The system may process information through n8n and related infrastructure providers. For trend analysis, the intended design may send the minimum necessary selected information to an external AI provider, which may include QwenCloud. The exact provider and data-sharing arrangement for Threads information have not been confirmed and must be reviewed against Meta's applicable terms before the feature is enabled.

Bytewise's workflow documentation also includes AI integrations for content generation and quality review. This does not by itself establish whether Threads-derived information is sent to those providers. The provider, data fields, purposes, and applicable provider terms must be confirmed before publication.

Service providers may process information on Bytewise's behalf or as described in their own terms. No provider retention, training, security, or deletion guarantee is made here unless separately confirmed.

5. Storage and retention

Bytewise does not intend to permanently store raw Threads trend-search results in Supabase. Search information may be processed temporarily in n8n. n8n execution history may retain workflow inputs and outputs, depending on workflow settings and execution-history configuration. The applicable retention settings and periods have not been confirmed for this draft.

Supabase may hold broader operational records, including persona information, approved topics, generated content, queue records, publishing statuses, and related data. These records are distinct from raw search results. Actual retention periods, backups, and deletion behavior depend on the configured services and require confirmation.

6. Credentials and security

API credentials and access tokens are used to authorize connections and requests. Their specific storage locations, access controls, protection, rotation, and removal procedures depend on the connected service and Bytewise configuration and must be verified before this policy is published.

The specific technical and organizational safeguards depend on the connected services and Bytewise configuration and have not been fully documented for this draft. No method of transmission or storage can be represented as risk-free.

7. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live and the laws that apply, you may be able to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information Bytewise holds about you, or exercise other privacy rights. To make a request, follow the Data Deletion Instructions or contact Bytewise using the details below.

You can also revoke the Bytewise app's access through the relevant Meta account settings. Revoking access may prevent connected workflows from continuing to use Meta APIs. See the deletion page for general instructions.

8. Contact

Privacy and deletion requests: bytewiserpersona@yahoo.com. This address must be confirmed as Bytewise's approved and monitored privacy contact before publication.
